THE STUDIO

A video studio. A different way to create.

ALTERSTATE is an independent, AI-first video production studio for brands, companies and creative teams.

Creativity comes first.

Our experience comes from creating online video with over 25 million views. That work has taught us how to capture attention, build a story and give people a reason to keep watching. We bring that creative and storytelling experience to every project.

We start with what you want to say and how you want people to feel. The idea leads; the tools help us bring it to life.

More room for the idea.

New production tools let us express ideas in ways that once felt out of reach. A setting that would have been too costly to build. An image that would have needed a much larger crew. A visual direction we can now explore and refine as the story develops.

We combine AI-assisted production with creative direction, editing and sound to make those possibilities work for your film. More freedom to create, with less of the cost and coordination of traditional video production.

A clear process. From start to finish.

Bring us an early idea or a finished brief. Together, we find the story, agree on the visual direction and set the scope, schedule and price before production begins.

We guide the production, share work for your feedback and refine the film together. Then we finish the edit and sound, and deliver the agreed versions for your channels. You have a creative partner throughout, with clear decisions and an easy way to stay involved.
